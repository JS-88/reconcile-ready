AMAZON DATA USE

Your Amazon data, used only for your books.

Amazon is one of Reconcile Ready’s first planned sales-channel connections. We will connect through the Selling Partner API with your authorisation. This page lists exactly what we request and why.

UPDATED 18 NOVEMBER 2026

How the connection works

You authorise Reconcile Ready from Amazon Seller Central. We never see or store your Amazon password. You choose which marketplaces to connect and can revoke access at any time from Seller Central → Apps and Services → Manage Your Apps.

What we request, and why

API role Data Used for
Finance and Accounting Settlement reports, financial events, fees, refunds, reimbursements, reserve movements Explaining each payout and preparing matching entries for Xero
Inventory and Order Tracking Orders and order items (SKU, quantity, price, dates) Product-level sales and profitability
Tax Remittance (restricted) Order tax data and the ship-to location needed to determine VAT treatment Correct VAT amounts and tax codes on Xero entries and VAT reporting

We request only these roles. We do not request access to buyer messaging, shipping labels, listings management or advertising accounts.

Data flow

  1. Amazon → Reconcile Ready: data arrives over an encrypted API connection using your authorisation.
  2. Processing: transactions are categorised, mapped to your Xero accounts and matched to costs you provide. Buyer details are reduced to the minimum needed for VAT.
  3. Reconcile Ready → Xero: summarised settlement entries are posted to your Xero organisation after you review them. Buyer personal information is never sent to Xero.

Buyer personal information

Buyer information is used only to work out the correct tax treatment. It is never used to contact buyers, never shared, never used for marketing and never combined across sellers. It is deleted within 30 days of order delivery unless tax law requires us to keep it, in which case it stays encrypted and restricted until that obligation ends.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share Amazon data with third parties.
  • Use Amazon data to compete with Amazon or with any seller.
  • Contact buyers, or use buyer data for marketing.
  • Request more data or roles than a feature needs.

Disconnecting

Revoking access in Seller Central stops all data collection immediately. Data already received is deleted within 30 days of disconnection, or sooner on request to hello@reconcileready.app.
