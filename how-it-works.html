BEFORE YOU CONNECT

What to expect.

Is Reconcile Ready available now?+

The software is in development. This website explains the planned product and lets you explore sample interfaces. Live Amazon, eBay, Shopify and Xero connections are not available yet. Early-access enquiries are open: email hello@reconcileready.app.

Where will product costs come from?+

You will need to supply product purchase costs and relevant freight, duty and preparation charges. Channel data does not include your complete cost of goods or business overheads. Missing inputs will need to be visible in reporting.

Which countries, currencies and tax rules will be supported?+

Initial coverage is being defined. The GBP and UK examples on this site are illustrations, not a commitment to regional availability. Supported channel regions, currencies, Xero plans and tax treatments will be published before launch.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST CONNECTION

A little preparation.
A clearer starting point.

Use this checklist to understand what a future setup may need. No account access is requested by this preview website.

01

Your sales channels

Identify the Amazon, eBay and Shopify accounts, stores and regions you want to report on. Note which payment and fulfilment routes each channel uses.

02

Your Xero organisation

Know which organisation should receive entries, who can authorise access, and who will review account and tax mappings.

03

Your product costs

Gather purchase costs and relevant freight, duty and preparation charges. Make sure costs can be linked to your products.

04

Your reporting starting point

Decide the first period to review and identify anything already posted to your books. Historical coverage and changeover handling will be confirmed before launch.
