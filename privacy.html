PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, plainly explained.

How we handle information on this website and in the Reconcile Ready service, including data you authorise us to receive from Amazon and Xero.

UPDATED 18 NOVEMBER 2026

Who we are

Reconcile Ready is operated by J5 Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number [COMPANY NUMBER]), registered office [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]. Contact: hello@reconcileready.app.

We are the controller for website enquiries and account information. For Amazon and Xero data processed on a customer’s behalf, we act as processor for that customer. ICO registration: [ICO REGISTRATION NUMBER].

What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, work email, business name, area of interest and message when you use our website enquiry form. These details are delivered to our hello@reconcileready.app mailbox so we can respond.
  • Account information: name, email and business details for users of the service.
  • Amazon data you authorise: settlement and financial-event data, order and sales data, fees, refunds and the tax information needed for VAT reporting. See How we use Amazon data for the exact list.
  • Xero data you authorise: your chart of accounts, tax rates and the bank-account details needed to post and match entries.
  • Costs you enter: product, landed and advertising costs used for profitability reporting.

Why we use it

Only to provide the service you asked for: explaining Amazon payouts, preparing entries for your Xero organisation and calculating product profitability. Our lawful basis is performance of our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

We do not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use Amazon data to build profiles, for marketing, or to compete with Amazon or any seller. We do not combine one seller’s data with another’s.

Buyer personal information

Some Amazon tax reports contain buyer information such as a shipping address. We use only what is needed to determine the correct VAT treatment (typically the destination country and postcode). We never contact buyers, never use buyer data for marketing, and never disclose it except where the law requires.

Buyer personal information is deleted within 30 days of the order being delivered, unless a longer period is required by tax law. Where tax law requires retention, it is kept encrypted, access-restricted and used for no other purpose, then deleted when that obligation ends.

Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored on servers in the United Kingdom. We use a small number of service providers, each bound by a written data processing agreement:

  • IONOS (UK) – hosting and storage
  • Hostinger – business email
  • Amazon and Xero – the platforms you connect, under their own terms

We will update this list before adding any new provider that processes customer data.

How long we keep it

  • Amazon and Xero data: while your account is active. When you disconnect or close your account, it is deleted within 30 days.
  • Buyer personal information: as described above.
  • Enquiries: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Backups: overwritten on a rolling cycle of no more than 30 days.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can revoke Reconcile Ready’s access at any time from Amazon Seller Central (Apps and Services → Manage Your Apps) or from your Xero connected-apps settings. Revoking access stops all new data collection immediately. To have existing data deleted, email us and we will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or object to its use. Email hello@reconcileready.app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not use third-party tracking scripts. The service uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be emailed to account holders before they take effect.
