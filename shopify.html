PERMISSIONS & CONTROL

Store access with
clear boundaries.

The planned connection will use Shopify’s app authorisation and required read permissions. Order history and payment coverage depend on approved scopes, payment setup and the records Shopify makes available.

  • Historical order access may require additional approval
  • Shopify Payments detail applies only where Shopify Payments is used
  • Other gateways may provide different payout detail
